# Cari Perbedaannya — Pasar Malam, Batik & Sawah

A spot-the-difference game in three original Indonesian scenes, drawn entirely
with code: a night market under a crescent moon, a batik workshop, and a rice
terrace at sunrise. Seven differences hide in each scene; click one in either
panel to ring it in gold. A hint lantern (H) glows near a difference you
haven't found yet. Wrong clicks cost 10 points; clearing a scene fast earns a
time bonus. Play it with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080.
